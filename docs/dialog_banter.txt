================================================================================
ELMORIAN CRISIS - NPC DIALOG BANTER
================================================================================

This document contains dialog lines for house NPCs across the 5-day campaign.
Each NPC is assigned one of 5 personality archetypes at the start of each day.

ARCHETYPES:
- GRUFF    : Angry, short-tempered, confrontational
- NERVOUS  : Anxious, paranoid, speaks quickly
- FRIENDLY : Positive, warm, tries to stay hopeful
- SNOBY    : Dismissive, superior attitude, complains about everything
- CONFUSED : Disoriented, asks questions, doesn't understand the situation

================================================================================
DAY 1 - THREAT LEVEL: LOW
================================================================================
General mood: Mild annoyance, life continues mostly normally.
Context: New enforcement policies announced, but most see it as bureaucracy.

--- MOM'S INSTRUCTIONS (Restaurant Interior) ---

	"Good morning dear! I have prepared some food\n" \
	"for our neighbors. Can you please deliver\n" \
	"them for me?"
  
   "Don't be late for supper!"
--- MOM'S COMMENTARY (State of Affairs) ---

"Did you hear about that the enforcers are in town? Apparently they're here to help the police clean up the streets."

"Business has been slow. People are staying home more."

"Try not to worry, sweetie. Everything will be back to normal soon."

--- FOOD REJECTION (Wrong House) ---

GRUFF:
"I didn't order anything. You got the wrong house."
[Follow-up] "What, you deaf? I said WRONG HOUSE. Now scram!"
[Follow-up] "These delivery kids today... can't even read an address."

NERVOUS:
"Oh! Um, no, I don't think that's for me? Did someone send this?"
[Follow-up] "Are you sure? Let me check... no, no I definitely didn't order."
[Follow-up] "Sorry, sorry! Maybe try next door?"

FRIENDLY:
"Oh honey, I think there's been a mix-up! I didn't order anything today."
[Follow-up] "No worries though! Mistakes happen. Good luck finding the right house!"
[Follow-up] "Such a nice young person, working hard. Stay safe out there!"

SNOBY:
"Excuse me? I don't eat... whatever THAT is. Check your list again."
[Follow-up] "Honestly, the service quality these days is appalling."
[Follow-up] "I only order from establishments with proper credentials."

CONFUSED:
"Food? Did I order food? I don't remember ordering food..."
[Follow-up] "Wait, what day is it? Maybe I did order something..."
[Follow-up] "No, no... I think you want someone else. Maybe."

--- WORLD COMMENTARY ---

GRUFF:
"Did you see those new 'enforcers' walking around? Waste of taxpayer money."
"Another day, another stupid new policy from the Empire."

NERVOUS:
"Have you noticed more patrols lately? I'm sure it's nothing..."
"They say it's just temporary measures. That's what they always say."

FRIENDLY:
"Beautiful day, isn't it? Despite all the news, gotta appreciate the small things!"
"I heard they're just doing routine checks. Nothing to worry about!"

SNOBY:
"The neighborhood is getting so crowded with all these officials."
"I suppose SOME regulation is necessary for the lesser districts."

CONFUSED:
"Why are there so many people in uniforms? Is there a parade?"
"Someone mentioned new laws? I really should read the news more..."

DAY 2 - THREAT LEVEL: MODERATE
================================================================================
General mood: Growing concern, news of workplace arrests.
Context: Reports of people being detained at their jobs. Tension rising.

--- MOM'S INSTRUCTIONS (Restaurant Interior) ---

"Another delivery today, sweetie. Be extra careful out there, come straight home when you're done."


--- MOM'S COMMENTARY (State of Affairs) ---

"Your father called. He said he talked to the chief of police. The chief said he's got a guy on the inside making sure they don't arrest kids."

"Some of the delivery drivers never showed up for their shifts today. I hope they're ok."

"Promise me you'll come straight home, okay?"

--- FOOD REJECTION (Wrong House) ---

GRUFF:
"Not mine. And keep your voice down - enforcers are everywhere."
[Follow-up] "You think I ordered food at a time like this? Get lost!"
[Follow-up] "Everyone's losing their minds and you're delivering takeout?"

NERVOUS:
"No no no, that's not mine! Please, just... just go, okay?"
[Follow-up] "I can't have people coming to my door right now!"
[Follow-up] "What if they think I'm... never mind, just please leave!"

FRIENDLY:
"That's not mine, sweetie, but be careful out there, okay?"
[Follow-up] "Did you hear about the arrests? Stay safe, dear."
[Follow-up] "Times are getting strange. Look after yourself!"

SNOBY:
"I haven't ordered anything. Unlike SOME people, I stay home where it's safe."
[Follow-up] "Running around making deliveries... how quaint."
[Follow-up] "At least MY papers are in order."

CONFUSED:
"Hmm? No, I don't think so. Did my neighbor order this?"
[Follow-up] "Actually, where IS my neighbor? Haven't seen them today..."
[Follow-up] "Lots of people seem to be staying inside lately."

--- WORLD COMMENTARY ---

GRUFF:
"They took three people from the factory yesterday. Just dragged them out."
"This 'enforcement' is getting out of hand. Someone needs to push back."

NERVOUS:
"My coworker didn't show up today. No one knows where she went."
"I heard they're checking documents at random now. RANDOM!"

FRIENDLY:
"My friend's husband got detained at work. She's devastated, poor thing."
"We have to stay positive. I'm sure this will all blow over soon..."

SNOBY:
"Well, if people kept their documents in order, there wouldn't be problems."
"I heard it's only affecting the lower sectors. Not OUR concern."

CONFUSED:
"People are talking about arrests? What do you mean, arrests?"
"I thought the Empire was supposed to protect us?"

DAY 3 - THREAT LEVEL: HIGH
================================================================================
General mood: Fear and anger building. Community fracturing.
Context: Protests beginning, neighbors disappearing overnight.

--- MOM'S INSTRUCTIONS (Restaurant Interior) ---

"We're barely hanging on hun. I really need you to help out the shop today."

--- MOM'S COMMENTARY (State of Affairs) ---

"Protestors gathered outside our shop this morning. The enforcers came bay and tear gassed them all. What nerve! They were protesting peacefully!"

"Please be careful. You're all I have left."

--- FOOD REJECTION (Wrong House) ---

GRUFF:
"Wrong house! And if you're smart, you'll stay off the streets!"
[Follow-up] "There's a protest down at the square. Things are getting ugly."
[Follow-up] "The Empire's gone too far this time. Mark my words."

NERVOUS:
"NOT MINE! Oh stars, please don't knock so loud!"
[Follow-up] "They came for the Velnars last night. Just... gone."
[Follow-up] "I haven't slept in days. Every noise could be THEM."

FRIENDLY:
"Oh, that's not mine, but... you shouldn't be out here alone."
[Follow-up] "The Hendricks family across the street - vanished yesterday."
[Follow-up] "Some people are gathering to protest. Maybe there's still hope."

SNOBY:
"Wrong address. Frankly, I'm surprised you're still working at all."
[Follow-up] "Those protesters are going to make things worse for everyone."
[Follow-up] "If people would just COMPLY, we wouldn't have these problems."

CONFUSED:
"What? No, that's not... wait, did you hear that noise outside?"
[Follow-up] "Where did the Martins go? Their house has been dark for days."
[Follow-up] "I don't understand what's happening anymore."

--- WORLD COMMENTARY ---

GRUFF:
"Half my street is empty now. WHERE ARE THEY TAKING PEOPLE?"
"I'm done being quiet. If they come for me, I'll go down fighting."

NERVOUS:
"I heard screaming from next door last night. This morning - silence."
"I've packed a bag. Just in case. You should too."

FRIENDLY:
"Some brave souls are protesting. I wish I was as courageous."
"We have to stick together. That's the only way through this."

SNOBY:
"The property values on this street are going to plummet."
"I've filed complaints with every office. Not that anyone listens."

CONFUSED:
"So many empty houses now. Did everyone go on vacation?"
"Why won't anyone tell me what's going on?"

DAY 4 - THREAT LEVEL: SEVERE
================================================================================
General mood: Terror. Open violence against dissenters.
Context: Enforcers breaking into homes, protesters attacked in streets.

--- MOM'S INSTRUCTIONS (Restaurant Interior) ---

"I don't want you going out today. I'm just waiting for the substitute driver for Mr. Bito."

--- MOM'S COMMENTARY (State of Affairs) ---

"I heard enforcers kicking down the neighbors door last night. I couldn't sleep after."

"They're going door to door. If they knock here, run and hide in the closet. Don't answer the door for any strangers."

"I love you. I need you to know that. Whatever happens."

--- FOOD REJECTION (Wrong House) ---

GRUFF:
"WHAT? No - get inside, quick, before they see you!"
[Follow-up] "Enforcers beat a group of protesters this morning. In broad daylight."
[Follow-up] "This isn't enforcement anymore. This is occupation."

NERVOUS:
"*whispers* Wrong house, wrong house! They're watching!"
[Follow-up] "They broke down the door across the street. Just smashed it in."
[Follow-up] "I've barricaded everything. Please just go!"

FRIENDLY:
"Not mine, dear, but please - come inside for a moment. It's not safe."
[Follow-up] "They're attacking anyone on the streets now."
[Follow-up] "I pray for us all. What have we become?"

SNOBY:
"For the last time - WRONG! And close that door, you're letting fear in!"
[Follow-up] "This is what happens when the rabble makes trouble."
[Follow-up] "...Although even I must admit, this seems... excessive."

CONFUSED:
"I... I don't know. I don't know anything anymore."
[Follow-up] "Why are they hurting people? I don't understand."
[Follow-up] "My daughter was supposed to visit. She never came."

--- WORLD COMMENTARY ---

GRUFF:
"They're kicking in doors without warrants. Without REASON."
"This is war. They just haven't officially declared it yet."

NERVOUS:
"I watched them drag someone from their home. They were screaming."
"I want to help, but I'm so scared. I'm so scared."

FRIENDLY:
"Even in darkness, we must be lights for each other."
"I've been hiding neighbors in my basement. Don't tell anyone."

SNOBY:
"I... I never thought they'd come to THIS district."
"Perhaps I was wrong about some things. Perhaps."

CONFUSED:
"They took my neighbor. She was crying for help. I didn't know what to do."
"Is this still the same world I woke up in?"

[Follow-up] "When everything falls apart, you finally see what's important."

--- WORLD COMMENTARY ---

GRUFF:
"Look at these empty streets. They took almost everyone."
"The Galactic Council will hear about this. And if they don't act, WE WILL."

NERVOUS:
"I'm done being afraid. Fear doesn't keep you safe. It keeps you silent."
"We're all meeting at the square. Even if it's the last thing we do."

FRIENDLY:
"So many lost. But those of us left - we're a family now."
"The masks you're delivering... you're giving people hope. You're a hero."

SNOBY:
"My status, my wealth... none of it protected me. None of it protected THEM."
"I finally understand. We either stand together or fall alone."

CONFUSED:
"Everything makes sense now. Isn't that strange? In all this chaos..."
"The square. That's where hope is. I can feel it."

================================================================================
IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
================================================================================

1. Assign personality randomly to each NPC at start of day
2. Store personality in NPC data so it persists through the day
3. When player delivers to wrong house:
   - Show initial rejection line
   - If player interacts again, show follow-up lines
4. Random chance to show world commentary when entering/exiting houses
5. Commentary should match the day's events and NPC personality
6. On the last day (Day 5) your mother is gone, and she left the last box
   of food on the ground with a farewell note.

MOM'S DIALOG PROGRESSION:
- Day 1: Optimistic, thinks it's temporary, gives delivery instructions
- Day 2: Growing concern, mentions arrests, tells child to be careful
- Day 3: Frightened, mentions disappearances, prepares for worst case
- Day 4: Terrified, gives survival instructions, expresses love
- Day 5: GONE - only a letter remains (the note triggers automatically)

Mom should comment on state of affairs when:
- Player returns to restaurant between deliveries
- Player talks to her before leaving on the day's delivery
- Random chance during dialog (pick one line from commentary)

DIALOG TRIGGER PRIORITY:
1. Mom's delivery instructions (first interaction of the day)
2. Mom's state commentary (subsequent interactions)
3. Food delivery response (accept/reject at houses)
4. Follow-up if rejected (on second interaction)
5. World commentary from NPCs (random chance)
